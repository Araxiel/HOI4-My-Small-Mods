Following small changes:

[h3]General changes:[/h3]
[list]
[*] Halved "Send Attache" cost.
[*] Increased yearly population growth to 160%.
[*] Increased Intel from alliance factor.
[*] Increased max naval factories for capital ships from 5 to 10.

[h3]Changes to licensing:[/h3]
[list]
[*] Halved licensing cost (both initial and gradual increase).
[*] Buffed licensed equipment production from 75% to 125%.
[*] Halved production cost increase for each year of difference between actual and latest equipment.
[*] Doubled the production cost of licensed equipment if NOT in faction with licensee.
[*] Changed XP cost of upgrading licensed equipment from 200% down to 150%.
[/list]
As such, licensing equipment to a faction member has been greatly buffed, and licensing to a non-faction member has been slightly buffed.
