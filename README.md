# My Small Mods
A repo with a bunch of my small Hearts of Iron 4 mods I made and personally use, and which are too small or specific to bother uploading and publishing. <br>
Each folder should have it's own readme.

## arax_BetterMedals
Cheaper and better medals.
<br>[Readme](https://github.com/Araxiel/HOI4-My-Small-Mods/blob/main/arax_BetterMedals/readme.md)

## arax_DefinesAdjustments
Just a bunch of adjustments, mostly various small buffs to various things.
<br>[Readme](https://github.com/Araxiel/HOI4-My-Small-Mods/blob/main/arax_DefinesAdjustments/readme.txt)

## arax_PersonalPatch
A potpourrie of random stuff and patches and *fixes*.<br>
Contains decisions to fix bordergore or weird edgecases or what I consider oversights in some NFs or decisions.<br>
Changes to
- Faction range
- Some generic MIOs
- A couple of country-specific decisions.

[Readme](https://github.com/Araxiel/HOI4-My-Small-Mods/blob/main/arax_PersonalPatch/readme.md)

## arax_FactionRestrictions
A mod that gives factions more restrictions by default, primarly by forcing most factions to be region/continent-locked by default.
Also adds some more region locks, i.e. "Roman Empire" which includes both Europe and Africa.
